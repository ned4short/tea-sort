How to play Tea Sort Puzzle

Tea Sort Puzzle is a free, relaxing pouring puzzle you play right in your browser. Every cup starts with a mix of teas stacked in layers. Your job is to pour them around until each cup holds just one kind of tea.

  • Tap a cup to pick it up, then tap another cup to pour.
  • You can only pour onto the same tea, or into an empty cup.
  • A cup holds four layers and can't be filled past the brim.
  • Solve the level when every cup holds one kind of tea.
  • Stuck? Use Undo, Restart, or add one extra cup per level.
  • Every level has a par: the fewest pours it can be solved in. Finish at par for ★★★, within a few moves for ★★, or any finish for ★.
  • 80 levels, from 3 teas up to 12. Your progress and stars are saved in your browser.

The golden ticket

Find and click the golden ticket to win a free cup of tea at the Tea Blossom. It can only appear on the pop-up after you complete a level, and not every time, so keep solving levels and watch for it. To redeem it, show the ticket in the game on your phone at the Tea Blossom (screenshots can't be redeemed), and a staff member will mark it as redeemed. Each golden ticket expires 30 days after you find it. Once a ticket has been used or has expired, the next one is twice as hard to find.
