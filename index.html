How to play Tea Sort Puzzle

Tea Sort Puzzle is a free, relaxing pouring puzzle you play right in your browser. Every cup starts with a mix of teas stacked in layers. Your job is to pour them around until each cup holds just one kind of tea.

  • Tap a cup to pick it up, then tap another cup to pour.
  • You can only pour onto the same tea, or into an empty cup.
  • A cup holds four layers and can't be filled past the brim.
  • Solve the level when every cup holds one kind of tea.
  • Stuck? Use Undo, Restart, or add one extra cup per level.
  • 80 levels, from 3 teas up to 12. Your progress is saved in your browser.
