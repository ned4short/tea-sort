Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

No account, no personal information

Tea Sort Puzzle doesn't require an account and doesn't ask for or collect your name, email or any other personal information.

Your progress stays on your device

Your level progress, and your golden ticket if you find one, are saved in your own browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any web host, these services may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

Other websites

Links to nedairfilters.com and our other games go to separate websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Questions? Reach us through nedairfilters.com.
