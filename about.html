About Tea Sort Puzzle

by Erica Powers (built by Ned Games)

Tea Sort Puzzle is a free pouring puzzle for tea lovers and puzzle fans. Each level gives you a row of glass tea cups filled with mixed layers of tea. Pour from cup to cup until every cup holds a single kind of tea. There are 80 levels, starting with 3 teas and building up to 12. No download, no sign-up, and it works on phones, tablets and computers.

How to play

  1. Tap a cup to pick it up. It lifts so you know it's selected.
  2. Tap another cup to pour. The top tea pours across, along with any matching layers right under it.
  3. You can only pour onto the same tea, or into an empty cup.
  4. Each cup holds four layers. You can't pour past the brim.
  5. When every cup holds one kind of tea, the level is solved and the next one unlocks.

Tips

  • Keep an empty cup free for as long as you can. It's your most useful tool.
  • Look a few pours ahead before you fill your last empty cup.
  • Made a wrong move? Undo as many times as you like, or Restart the level.
  • Really stuck? Each level lets you add one extra empty cup.
  • Chasing stars? Each level has a par, the fewest pours that solve it. Finish at par for three stars, within a few moves for two, and any finish earns one.

The 12 teas

Green Tea, Black Tea, Chai, Hibiscus, Butterfly Pea, Chamomile, Peppermint, Rooibos, Lavender, Earl Grey, Rose and Milk Oolong. Every tea also has its own small symbol, so the puzzle is playable for people who have trouble telling some colors apart.

The golden ticket

Find and click the golden ticket to win a free cup of tea at the Tea Blossom. The ticket can only appear on the pop-up after you complete a level, and not every time, so the more levels you solve, the more chances you get. If you find it, show the ticket in the game on your phone at the Tea Blossom. Screenshots can't be redeemed. A staff member will tap "Redeem ticket" to mark it as used. Each golden ticket expires 30 days after you find it, and the expiry date is printed on the ticket. After a ticket has been redeemed or has expired, your next golden ticket is twice as hard to find, and each one after that is twice as hard again.
